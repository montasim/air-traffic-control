# Store badges

Official English badge artwork (Microsoft and Snap retrieved 2026-10-02; Google Play retrieved 2026-10-22):

- `google-play.svg`: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/?folder=86718 — Google’s `GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg`. Google Play and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC.
- `microsoft-store.svg`: https://get.microsoft.com/images/en-us%20dark.svg — official static badge documented at https://github.com/microsoft/app-store-badge
- `snap-store.svg`: https://snapcraft.io/static/images/badges/en/snap-store-black.svg — Canonical's badge, documented at https://snapcraft.io/about/publicise. Snapcraft logo © Canonical Limited; CC BY-ND 2.0 UK, https://github.com/canonical/snap-store-badges/blob/master/LICENSE.md.

The Google Play badge’s outer border was removed at the project owner’s request. The Snap badge’s outer border was removed and its background corner radius matched proportionally to the Microsoft badge at the project owner’s request. The Google Play and Snap backgrounds use the Microsoft badge’s `#202020` background color at the project owner’s request. The badges retain their original proportions and link to this game's store listings. They are bundled locally so the artwork remains available offline; visiting a store requires a connection.
