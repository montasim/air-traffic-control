Privacy Policy

Air Traffic Control · Last updated October 4, 2026

Air Traffic Control is a single-player game by Montasim, available on the web, as a desktop app, and as an Android app on Google Play. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small record on your own device: your best scores per airfield, career rank, achievements, and settings such as sound volume and the runway-end option. On Android and the desktop app this lives in the app's private storage; in a browser it lives in that browser's storage for the game's site.

  • This record never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Permissions and network access

The Android app requests no permissions. It plays fully offline and does not contact any server while you play. Updates are delivered by Google Play.

Links to other websites

The game may link to outside websites (for example, the developer's website). Those sites have their own privacy policies; this policy covers only the game.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
