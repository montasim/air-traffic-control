Privacy Policy

Air Traffic Control · Last updated October 7, 2026

Air Traffic Control is a single-player game by Montasim, available on the web, as a desktop app, and as an Android app on Google Play. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

Ordinary play stays on your device. If you choose Tower Challenge, the game sends the route commands needed to verify your score and uses a random identifier and generated callsign to place that score in the global standings. There are no player accounts, advertisements, or analytics, and we do not sell personal information.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small record on your own device: your best scores per airfield, career rank, achievements, and settings such as sound volume and the runway-end option. On Android and the desktop app this lives in the app's private storage; in a browser it lives in that browser's storage for the game's site.

  • Career data never leaves your device. Tower Challenge identity and cached standings are kept separately from it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Optional Tower Challenge

Tower Challenge is optional and requires an internet connection. When you participate, the game sends a randomly generated credential, your selected portrait or landscape division, and the route commands from that ranked flight. The server replays those commands to verify the score. After three verified runs, the game may ask two optional multiple-choice fairness questions. The public board shows only a generated callsign, rank, and score.

  • Raw route-command replays are removed after 30 days. Verified score records, the random identifier, generated callsign, and operational timestamps may be kept so the standings and abuse controls continue to work.
  • The hosting provider may process network information such as an IP address in security and request logs under its own retention practices.
  • Ranked results never change your local career, records, achievements, or settings. You can continue ordinary play offline.
  • If you answer the optional fairness questions, the selected answers are stored with the anonymous challenge identity for up to 90 days. The game does not request free-text feedback.
  • Clearing the app or site data removes your local credential. A later entry will receive a new generated identity; old public scores may remain under the previous callsign.

Permissions and network access

The Android app requests internet access for the optional Tower Challenge. Ordinary flights and locally bundled game assets remain playable offline. Updates are delivered by Google Play.

Links to other websites

The game may link to outside websites (for example, the developer's website). Those sites have their own privacy policies; this policy covers only the game.

Children

The game does not ask for a name, email address, age, or account. Children can use ordinary offline play without sending challenge data. If a child uses Tower Challenge, the same anonymous competition data described above is processed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
