## Store metadata

Category: Games

Summary: Draw flight paths, land aircraft, and keep the skies safe.

Description:

Take charge of the skies in Air Traffic Control, a relaxed-looking arcade game that gets busier with every arrival. Draw routes with your mouse, finger, or pen, guide aircraft to matching runways and helipads, and keep traffic safely separated.

Every safe landing earns a point. A collision or an aircraft leaving the sector ends your shift—so plan ahead and reroute when traffic gets close.

Features
- Nine airfields, from quiet regional airports to military, cargo, passenger, and rescue locations.
- Three aircraft types: airliners, commuters, and helicopters.
- Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulties, with separate best scores.
- Seven career ranks, progressive airfield unlocks, and nine achievements.
- A guided practice flight to learn the controls.
- Offline play, local saved progress, and adjustable sound and effects.

No account or internet connection is needed to play. Your career and records stay on your device. Drawing routes requires a mouse, touch screen, or pen.
