## Full description (max 4000)

Take charge of the tower. Draw a path for each arriving aircraft, line it up with the right runway end or helipad, and keep every flight apart as the sky gets busier.

Air Traffic Control is a calm, readable flight-routing game that is easy to learn and hard to master. Liners, commuter planes, and helicopters each need their own landing spot. Plan clean routes, avoid conflicts, and see how many safe landings you can make in one shift.

TWELVE AIRFIELDS
• Start at Saltmarsh Gateway and River Bend, then earn your way to deserts, river valleys, military and business fields, a busy passenger hub, a cargo junction, and an island rescue station.
• Frost Crossing: two runways cross in a snowbound archipelago.
• Carrier Coast: liners land on the shore while commuters land on a carrier offshore.
• Blue Water: open ocean and one carrier deck, with axial and angled landing lanes.

A CAREER TO GROW INTO
• Rise from Control Trainee to Air Boss and unlock new airfields along the way.
• Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.
• Earn achievements and beat your best score on every airfield.

MADE FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
• Smooth touch route drawing, in portrait or landscape.
• Optional two-end landing: let aircraft use either end of each runway.
• Watch landed aircraft roll out, taxi along the guide lines, and park at their stands.

PLAY ANYWHERE, NO STRINGS ATTACHED
• Works fully offline.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
• No personal data collected. Your progress stays on your device.
