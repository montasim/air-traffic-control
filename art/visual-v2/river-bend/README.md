# River Bend terrain plate — visual v2

These three terrain-only plates form the River Bend visual proof. The active
viewport loads one matching WebP; the runway, taxiways, apron, helipad,
buildings, landing geometry, aircraft, routes, warnings, and UI remain separate
code-native layers.

## Direction

- Mature realistic-cartoon countryside viewed directly from above.
- Dusty sage and desaturated olive ground with cool slate-blue water.
- Broad quiet value masses through the active routing area.
- Detailed hedgerows, fields, roads, and bridges stay near the perimeter and
  river bank.
- No aircraft signal colors, operational airport geometry, route lines, text,
  UI, or baked aircraft shadows.

## Provenance

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-01. The
approved River Bend concept was used only as a geography and painterly-style
reference. Landscape was generated first; portrait and square variants used
the accepted landscape treatment as their material and palette reference.

Runtime derivatives are Lanczos-scaled WebP files at quality 84. Source PNGs
remain here for future non-destructive art revisions and are not shipped.
